Operating model / four separate systems

Ownership, review, and access stay separate.

The Store can manage availability without changing who created the work or silently converting impact into currency.

01 / AUTHORSHIP

Authored work

Your stories, media, characters, and brand assets remain your intellectual property and creative work.

Ownership stays with creator
02 / GUARDIAN

Review state

Guardian checks source, attribution, privacy, suitability, and destination before publication.

Pending · approved · rejected
03 / ECONOMY

Anom Coin

A shared spendable balance used for eligible unlocks and tracked through Coin transactions.

Currency, not impact
04 / ACCESS

Server-confirmed use

A record states what an account may use after the server validates item state, balance, and access history.

Access, not ownership
